# FIRST MIX — Home override

The home page is a product introduction, not a course catalogue.

## Information hierarchy

1. Identify FIRST MIX as a zero-equipment, beginner-friendly interactive mixing experience.
2. State the outcome: hear two songs connect and complete a first transition.
3. Offer only three working entry points: first Mix, song-structure listening, and EQ transition.
4. Keep unreleased lessons off the home screen; they belong in the future course page.

## Visual direction

- Preserve generous negative space on phone screens.
- Use one cool accent family only: ink black, warm off-white, powder periwinkle, and neutral grey.
- Do not pair red with dusty blue in the interface.
- Use the hand-drawn DJ only as the home hero; functional screens should rely on layout and controls.
- Handwriting is limited to the FIRST MIX wordmark. Functional copy uses the system sans family.

## Interaction

- All three feature cards are at least 48px high.
- The first Mix is visually primary; the other two remain clearly available.
- Avoid autoplay and keep the sound-start control explicit.
